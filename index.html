The Institution

Sangeet Vidya Niketan is a classical Indian performing arts institution and centre of excellence, offering rigorous training alongside UGC-recognised degrees in music and dance.

A divine space where tradition, discipline and artistic expression come together, SVN nurtures young artists through dedicated mentorship, academic learning and meaningful performance opportunities.
